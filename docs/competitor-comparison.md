# Competitor Comparison

**Date:** 2026-04-05

## Theatre Logging Apps

| App | Log | Rate | Review | Work/Production Split | Discovery | Social | Coverage | Platform |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Show-Score** | Track only | 0-100 (now 5-star emoji) | Structured ("See it if...") | No | NYC/London | Follows, feed | NYC, London | Web |
| **BroadwayWorld My Shows** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Partial | Listings | Limited | Broadway-centric, expanding | Web |
| **Mezzanine** | Yes (diary) | Stars | Yes | Yes (production screens) | Limited | Follow friends | ~1,500 productions (40yr Broadway/WE) | iOS only |
| **Aklaim** | Yes (performance-level) | Stars | Yes | Partial (musical-focused) | Browse | Community | Musicals, Broadway/WE | iOS/Android |
| **Theatregoer** | Yes (detailed) | Yes | Notes | No | No | No | User-driven (any show) | iOS |
| **StagePort** | Yes | Yes | Mini-reviews | No | No | Compare friends | Unknown | Unknown |
| **TheaterLog** | Yes | Unknown | Unknown | No | No | No | User-driven | Unknown |

## What Each Does Well

| App | Strength |
|---|---|
| **Show-Score** | Structured review format ("See it if / Don't see it if") makes reviews actionable. Discovery by city + type is practical. |
| **BroadwayWorld** | Backed by the largest English-language theatre database (decades of productions worldwide). |
| **Mezzanine** | Closest to Letterboxd model. Programme scan for frictionless logging. Map-based venue stats. |
| **Aklaim** | Performance-level logging (specific date/time). Calendar view. Per-seat-type tracking. |
| **Theatregoer** | Maximum flexibility (user enters all data). Ticket attachment. Spreadsheet import. Price/seat tracking. |

## Gaps Across All Competitors

- No app has the full Work + Production hierarchy with a personal diary
- No app combines logging, stats, and discovery in one place
- Social features are weak or nonexistent across the board
- Database coverage is limited to Broadway/West End in most cases
- None match Letterboxd's polish or community scale

## How People Track Theatre Without an App

| Method | What It Reveals |
|---|---|
| **Spreadsheets** (most common) | People care most about: date, venue, rating, who they went with, ticket price |
| **Airtable** | Sort/filter matters. Ticket acquisition method is part of the experience. |
| **Notion** | "Want to see" list alongside "have seen" is not an afterthought |
| **Google Calendar** | Date + title is the irreducible minimum |
| **Physical notebooks/programmes** | Demand for "capture the programme" flow |
| **Show-Score reviews (as diary)** | People want a record that is *theirs*. Private log is table stakes. |
